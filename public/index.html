01 The practice

Not introductions. Delivery.

Skystone exists to do one thing well: bring the capability of China’s most accomplished firms into the work of Gulf principals — across real estate and construction, artificial intelligence, and advanced manufacturing.

A decade inside Dubai’s most exacting institutions sits behind the practice, alongside longstanding relationships across Mainland China. Native Mandarin fluency and deep ties within China’s construction, technology and manufacturing sectors are what make cross-border execution land.

We advise principals and family offices on capital deployment, partner selection and end-to-end implementation. The work is retained, private and selective — so you never have to navigate it alone.

03 Process

Four stages, over long horizons.

  1. I

    Stage one: Initial Consultation

    A single 90-minute meeting at our office or yours. No charge, no obligation.

  2. II

    Stage two: Position Paper

    A written brief: opportunity, capital structure, partner shortlist and a clear go / no-go recommendation.

  3. III

    Stage three: Diligence & Partner Engagement

    Counterparties engaged, terms negotiated, specifications locked. Chinese partners brought in early — for cost, buildability or technology fit.

  4. IV

    Stage four: Implementation & Oversight

    On-the-ground presence, monthly reports and quarterly reviews — through delivery and the post-completion period.
